Sequential Logistics Process

The Warehouse Operations Journey

From the moment freight touches our inbound apron to final carrier departure, each inventory milestone follows strict operational protocols.

01

Goods Received

Inbound docking, manifest cross-check, seal inspection, and initial receiving documentation.

02

Quality & Quantity Check

Physical carton counting, SKU verification, barcode generation, and quarantine clearance.

03

Putaway & Storage

Reach-truck transport to assigned high-bay rack locations with RF scan bin confirmation.

04

Picking & Packing

Sales order batch picking, protective packaging, item kitting, and shipping label printing.

05

Dispatch

Outbound pallet staging, carrier handoff, Bill of Lading confirmation, and linehaul departure.
