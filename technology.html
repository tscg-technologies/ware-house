Precision Verification

Barcode & Scanning Concepts

Paperless operations eliminate manual recording errors. Every physical transition requires a dual-scan: confirming the item SKU barcode and the physical bay aperture barcode.

This dual-verification ensures items cannot be put away into the wrong location without triggering an immediate system warning, preserving total inventory accuracy.
